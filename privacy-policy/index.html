Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

Techinoid Solutions (PVT) LTD built the Poster AI app — an AI poster maker that turns a short brief into a finished poster. This Privacy Policy explains what the app sends to our servers, what it keeps on your device, and the choices you have. It covers the Poster AI mobile app only. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

In this release: the app talks to a development server over an unencrypted connection. Until that moves to HTTPS, treat a brief as something that could be read in transit, and keep personal or confidential details out of it.

1. Information Collection

What the app sends us
Making a poster means sending us the brief you wrote. That is the only thing that leaves your device, and it is sent only when you ask for a poster.

  • Your idea, and the category, audience, tone and style you chose. These are combined into one brief that our generation service writes and lays out the poster from.
  • Text you change in the editor, when you ask for the poster to be rendered again.

What we never ask for
Poster AI has no accounts and no sign-in, so there is nothing to register and nothing for us to tie your briefs to.

  • No name, email address, phone number or payment details.
  • No contacts, location or microphone access.
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs.

Analytics and tracking
There are none. The app ships with no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries. We do not profile you, track you across apps or websites, and we have nothing to sell to data brokers.

2. What Stays on Your Device

Your work is kept locally, in storage private to the app. We cannot read it.

  • Drafts and the posters rendered from them, saved in the app’s own documents folder.
  • Your theme and language choices.
  • Deleting a draft deletes its files. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app has stored.

3. Photos and Sharing

Both are things you start, and both stay under your control.

  • Saving a poster asks for permission to add to your photo library the first time. The app only adds images — it never reads or browses what is already there.
  • Sharing hands the poster to the system share sheet. Whatever you share it to is then governed by that app’s own privacy policy.

4. How Briefs Are Handled

Your brief is processed by our generation service to produce the poster, and the result is held long enough for the app to download the rendered image.

We do not sell personal data
We do not sell briefs or the posters made from them, and we do not use them to advertise to you.

5. Children’s Privacy

Poster AI is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been provided, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

6. AI-Generated Content

Poster text and layouts in Poster AI are generated using artificial intelligence. Generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for some purposes, so please review a poster before you publish or share it.

7. Your Choices

You can clear what the app holds at any time, without asking us.

  • Delete any draft from the Drafts screen.
  • Withdraw photo-library access in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything it has stored.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected with a new Effective Date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy from time to time to stay informed about how we protect your information.
